Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 22, 2026

Buttercup is a private space for two people to share wishes, promises, to-dos, and memories. This policy explains what we collect, why, and what we don't.

What we collect

  • Your name — entered by you when you set up the app.
  • Your Apple ID identifier — a stable token from Sign in with Apple. We use it to recognize you across reinstalls. We never receive your email unless you choose Apple's "Share My Email" option.
  • Items you create — the text of your wishes, promises, to-dos, memories, and any photos you attach.
  • Your pair code — the 6-character code linking your account to your partner's.
  • Push notification token — Apple's APNs token, so we can send you partner notifications.
  • Purchase status — whether you have an active Buttercup Pro subscription, verified through Apple's StoreKit.

What we don't collect

  • We don't track you across other apps or websites.
  • We don't use third-party analytics or ad networks.
  • We don't share your data with advertisers.
  • We don't sell your data, ever.

How we use it

The data above is used only to deliver the app's features: keeping your account, syncing your shared space with your partner, sending you push notifications when your partner does something, and verifying your subscription status.

Where it lives

Your data is stored on encrypted servers hosted by Railway (US/EU regions). Photos are stored on Cloudflare R2. All connections use TLS. Within Buttercup, only you and your paired partner can see your shared items.

Deletion

You can delete your account at any time from Settings → Reset everything. This permanently removes your account, items, and photos from our servers. Deletion is irreversible.

Children

Buttercup is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect data from anyone under 13.

Changes

If we update this policy, we'll change the "Last updated" date and post the new version at this URL. Significant changes will be surfaced in the app.
